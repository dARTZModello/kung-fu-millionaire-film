a neon cyberpunk martial artist woman with magenta-pink hair with cyan streaks, glowing green eyes, neon LED tube outline only, corset top, platform boots, dynamic kung fu or dance pose, electric cyan and neon magenta lighting

Constants (non-negotiable)

  • Style / Medium. Neon LED tube outline only. Pure glowing tubes. No solid fills, no realistic skin, no photoreal faces. High contrast, film-still energy but pure neon graphic language.
  • Hair. Glossy magenta-pink to purple blunt bob or flowing with electric cyan streaks or accents. Can vary length slightly per look but stays in the magenta/cyan family.
  • Eyes. Glowing green. Always. Bright, electric.
  • Outfit base. Corset top with HEX LAB hexagon patterns, platform combat boots. Variants allowed (armor plates, dance cuts, different neon colors) as long as the tube outline holds.
  • Pose. Always dynamic: kung fu stances, flying kicks, punches, spinning strikes, dance hybrids, meditation with energy, power poses, horse stance. Never static or waiting.
  • Palette. Electric cyan, neon magenta, molten gold accents. Black background preferred. High contrast.
  • Brand. HEX LAB hex patterns, optional small KFM$ mark. Neon hexagon motifs.
  • Age / Build. Adult woman, athletic martial artist build. Never underage, never child-like, never soft influencer face.
  • Energy. Powerful, precise, electric. Millionaire martial artist. Coins, energy swirls, lightning optional for special plates.

Never List

  • Solid fills or realistic skin / photoreal faces
  • Off-palette colors (no random pastels, earth tones, or plain white fills)
  • Static standing poses, influencer smiles, or dead waiting faces
  • Anything that reads under-age or non-adult
  • Text or logos you did not specifically request (except optional small KFM$ / HEX LAB marks)
  • Breaking the neon tube outline style
  • Realistic lighting that kills the pure neon tube look
