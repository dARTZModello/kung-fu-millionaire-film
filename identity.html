Identity Text · Full Length & Wider

a neon cyberpunk martial artist woman with magenta-pink hair with cyan streaks, glowing green eyes, neon LED tube outline only, corset top, platform boots, dynamic kung fu or dance pose

Constants

  • Style / Medium. Neon LED tube outline only. Pure glowing tubes, no solid fills, no realistic skin, no photorealism. High contrast, film-still energy but pure neon graphic.
  • Hair. Glossy magenta-pink to purple blunt bob or flowing, with electric cyan streaks or accents.
  • Eyes. Glowing green. Always.
  • Outfit. Corset top with HEX LAB hexagon patterns, platform combat boots. White or neon variants acceptable for specific looks. Hex patterns preferred.
  • Pose. Always dynamic: kung fu stances, kicks, punches, dance hybrids, meditation, power poses. Never static or waiting.
  • Palette. Electric cyan, neon magenta, molten gold accents. Black background preferred.
  • Brand. HEX LAB, KFM$, neon hexagon motifs. Optional small KFM$ mark.
  • Age / Build. Adult woman, athletic martial artist build. Never underage or child-like.

Never List

  • Solid fills or realistic skin / photoreal faces
  • Off-palette colors (no random pastels or earth tones)
  • Static standing poses or influencer smiles
  • Anything that reads under-age
  • Text or logos you did not specifically request (except optional small KFM$ / HEX LAB marks)
  • Breaking the neon tube outline style

Reference Sheets

Use the character turnaround and pose sets from the main gallery as visual references. Attach them when the model supports image prompts. The style is neon LED tube outline only — keep every generation in that language.

Looks

Core look is fixed on head and style. Clothes can vary per film/world (different corset colors, armor hex plates, dance variants) as long as the neon tube outline and constants hold. See gallery for current key frames and ATL series.
